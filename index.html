01 / Research

Where physics meets
efficient computation.

01

Tensor networks & reduced-order modeling

Studying low-rank representations of turbulent fields using tensor-network methods, including matrix product states, to understand how physical information is retained under compression.

02

Computational turbulence

Working with direct numerical simulation data and flow statistics, with research spanning isotropic turbulence and turbulent reacting flows.

03

Quantum scientific computing

Exploring quantum algorithms for partial differential equations and fluid dynamics, alongside MPS-based state preparation and variational quantum methods.

02 / Selected publication

2026 · Preprint

A priori Assessment of Tensor-Network Encoding for Isotropic Turbulent Flows

Massen Esmaeili, Hirad Alipanah, Robert Pinkston, Peyman Givi, Daniel Livescu, Andrew J. Daley, Dieter Jaksch, and Juan José Mendoza-Arenas

Investigating how matrix product states represent turbulent velocity and scalar fields, and how compression affects reconstructed flow statistics.
